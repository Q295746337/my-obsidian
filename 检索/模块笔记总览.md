# 模块笔记总览

> 按文件夹统计笔记数量和清单。  
> 范围：`JavaSE`、`JavaWeb`、`MySQL`、`Redis`、`SSM框架`、`SpringBoot2`。

```dataview
TABLE length(rows) AS "笔记数", rows.file.link AS "笔记"
FROM "JavaSE" OR "JavaWeb" OR "MySQL" OR "Redis" OR "SSM框架" OR "SpringBoot2"
GROUP BY file.folder
SORT file.folder ASC
```
