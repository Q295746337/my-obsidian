# 别名总览

> 查看已配置的别名。别名只放在主笔记上，避免同一概念多处指向。  
> 范围：`JavaSE`、`JavaWeb`、`MySQL`、`Redis`、`SSM框架`、`SpringBoot2`、`JVM`、`JUC`、`AI`、`微服务`、`面试`、`八股文`、`黑马点评`。

```dataview
TABLE aliases AS "别名", file.folder AS "目录"
FROM "JavaSE" OR "JavaWeb" OR "MySQL" OR "Redis" OR "SSM框架" OR "SpringBoot2" OR "JVM" OR "JUC" OR "AI" OR "微服务" OR "面试" OR "八股文" OR "黑马点评"
WHERE aliases
SORT file.name ASC
```
